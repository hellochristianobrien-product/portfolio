Remi

Your operations on autopilot. Remi is the last operations hire you'll ever need: an AI teammate that learns how your company runs and keeps the work moving.

Visit Remi ↗

What it does

Remi learns from your email, calendar, docs and the tools you already run on, then works alongside the whole team. It pulls data together from every source, turns meetings into clear action items and runs multi-step workflows without being chased.

Why it matters

Operations is the work that keeps a company moving and the work nobody has time for. Remi reviews contracts, catches scope creep and flags things before they slip. The busywork that used to need a hire, handled.

My role

I drove the product vision, market research and delivery roadmap for an AI agent that automates operational and predictive work for service businesses. I built the agent evaluation framework, set the guardrails for when it acts on its own versus escalates to a human, and prompt engineered it through beta. I also partnered with the tech leads on model selection and the cost versus quality tradeoffs, turning technical outputs into product decisions.

Where it stands

Remi is in early access, with 200+ founders already running on it. Built with AI at Off/Menu. See it live at app.helloremi.ai.
